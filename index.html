NetStar Maintenance browser extension

Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026

NetStar Maintenance helps authorized dealership staff review maintenance requirements, recorded service evidence and prepaid maintenance coverage. This policy describes how the extension handles information.

Information processed

The extension reads vehicle identifiers (including VIN and FIN), vehicle equipment, mileage, service dates, repair-order and dealer identifiers, customer-pay and other service history, prepaid plan purchase and usage information, and maintenance documents from the user's authorized NetStar and StarTek sessions. Records or screenshots can contain personal information present in those applications.

The extension reads relevant application tab and launch URLs, including application user and dealer identifiers, to connect the maintenance workflow. It does not track unrelated browsing activity, keystrokes or usage analytics.

How information is used

Information is used to match maintenance sheets, calculate service intervals, show completion evidence and coverage limits, and produce reports requested by the user. The extension uses existing signed-in application sessions; it does not extract or store passwords, authentication cookies or authentication tokens.

Storage and retention

Maintenance reports are processed in the browser and kept in panel memory until cleared, refreshed or closed. Temporary launch context and tab associations are held in extension session storage; they are removed when the browser session ends or the extension reloads. The extension has no developer-hosted vehicle-data database or analytics service.

Files explicitly downloaded, printed or emailed are not removed when the extension is cleared. Those copies are subject to the user's organization, device and email-provider retention practices.

Problem reports and sharing

When the user chooses Report a Problem, the extension captures visible NetStar tab content and includes the available maintenance export. These attachments can contain vehicle identifiers and service records. The user's description, screenshot and export are transferred to the configured Outlook webmail draft when the user requests it. The draft is addressed to johnm@centennialimports.com. The user reviews and sends the email; the extension does not send it automatically. Users should review the screenshot and attachments before sending.

NetStar and StarTek receive requests needed to retrieve authorized records. The configured email service processes support drafts and sent messages. Support information sent to the developer is used to investigate and resolve the reported issue.

Data-use commitments

We do not sell user data, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. We use and transfer information only to provide or improve the extension's stated maintenance and support functions, or as required by law. We do not use information for unrelated purposes. Our handling of user data follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can clear the vehicle report, close the panel, remove the extension, and choose whether to export, print or send a problem report. For questions or requests to delete a support report sent to the developer, contact johnm@centennialimports.com. Deletion of copies held by your organization or email provider must be requested through those parties and may be subject to their retention requirements.
